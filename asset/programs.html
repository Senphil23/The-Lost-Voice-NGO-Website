Our programs

Practical support. Lasting possibility.

Our work combines mindset development, skills learning, and community support for people who need a fair chance to thrive.

Mindset & mentorship

TLVEI Mindset Academy

Monthly “Change Your Mind, Change Your Nation” seminars, one-to-one guidance, and community dialogue support confidence, resilience, and positive planning.

Five-week learning journey

  1. Self-discovery and personal value
  2. Purpose and vision
  3. Financial literacy: saving and budgeting
  4. Discipline and character
  5. Entrepreneurial thinking

The initiative’s program outline also describes three-hour seminars and mentor support for up to six months. Contact us for current dates and availability.

Practical vocational learning

Free Vocational Training

The program outline describes an intensive three-month course with practical, hands-on learning and no training or form fees for beneficiaries.

Training options

Tailoring and fashion design, hairdressing and makeover, catering and baking, ICT and digital skills, shoemaking and bag making, barbing and grooming, and agricultural entrepreneurship.

Starter kits are planned for outstanding graduates in selected courses, subject to program availability.

Family and community support

Single Mothers Rise

The initiative’s outline includes business grants and starter packs, monthly food and household support intended for 50–100 families, healthcare outreach, and a monthly sisterhood circle.

Grant amounts in the current plan range from ₦30,000 to ₦100,000. Activities and eligibility depend on available funding; contact us for current details.

Education support

Back to School: The Child of the Lost Voice

School support for children of beneficiaries, including help with school fees, uniforms, books, bags, and other learning materials, based on need and available resources.
