Who we are

Our story, rooted in dignity.

The Lost Voice Empowerment Initiative exists to help the forgotten be heard, supported, and equipped to move forward.

Since 2016

Born from a belief that no one should be overlooked.

The Lost Voice Empowerment Initiative was registered with Nigeria’s Corporate Affairs Commission in 2016. It was founded from a desire to see people whose needs are often overlooked listened to and supported.

After a period offline, the initiative is returning with a renewed commitment to community service, empowerment, and advocacy.

Our mission

Empowerment with dignity

To empower underprivileged people, especially single mothers and young people, through mindset transformation, free skills training, and holistic support that encourages dignity and self-reliance.

Our vision

A future where every voice matters

A Nigeria where every person, regardless of background, has the skills, mindset, and opportunity to thrive and contribute to their community.

Welcome home — you are not alone anymore

Dear beloved visitor,

Welcome to The Lost Voice Empowerment Initiative. If you are looking for support, or hoping to help someone who needs it, we are glad you found us.

I started this initiative in 2016 because I could not ignore the struggles of single mothers, young people without opportunities, and others whose needs were overlooked. I believed that if people were not being heard, we could help make space for their voices and connect them with support.

We worked quietly in our communities, then stepped offline for a season of restructuring and planning. We are now returning with renewed energy and a clearer commitment to service.

If you are a single mother, a young person, or someone facing hardship: you are seen, heard, and valued. Your past does not have to define your future. We hope you will find encouragement, practical skills, and community here.

If you are a donor, volunteer, or partner, thank you for your care and generosity. Your contribution of time, skills, or funds can help our work reach people who need support. Please contact us to learn about current programs and ways to contribute.

This is more than an organization to us; it is a community that believes no voice should be lost, and that changing mindsets can help change a nation.

Thank you for visiting and believing in this work. Let’s build a Nigeria where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

With love and hope,
Rawlings A. Ogie
Founder & Executive Director
